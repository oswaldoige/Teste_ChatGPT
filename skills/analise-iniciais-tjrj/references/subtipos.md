# Subtypes

## Common procedure against private defendant
- Base model: `${GABINETE_ACERVO}\00_PRIORITARIOS_AUTOMACAO\01 - DESPACHO INICIAL COMUM - AUD 334.docx`
- Use this only when the case is truly a common civil filing and there is no more suitable special act.
- If the author manifested disfavor toward the initial conciliation hearing, prefer the exact gabinete formula from the base model for the art. 334 passage and the citation command, with only factual adjustments strictly required by the case.

## Fazenda Publica
- Base model: `${GABINETE_ACERVO}\00_PRIORITARIOS_AUTOMACAO\02 - DESPACHO INICIAL FAZENDA PUBLICA.docx`
- Keep the Fazenda-specific regime: personal citation, service through the representation body, and 30-day answer period under arts. 242, paragrafo 3o, 247, III, 335, and 183 of the CPC.
- Preserve the Fazenda-specific wording of the base model whenever it fits, instead of rewriting it into a generic citation order.

## Execution of extrajudicial title
- Base model: `${GABINETE_ACERVO}\00_PRIORITARIOS_AUTOMACAO\03 - DESPACHO INICIAL EXECUCAO EXTRAJUDICIAL.docx`
- Check title adequacy, amount, costs, citation for payment, and art. 827 honorarios.

## Usucapiao
- Do not jump to a generic citation act.
- First check property description, registry data, confrontants, public entities, chain of possession, and whether the initial petition is complete enough for regular processing.

## 3o Nucleo 4.0 water and sewer cases
- Prioritize the specialized folder:
  `${GABINETE_ACERVO}\NUCLEO 4.0`
- For the first manifestation after redistribution, start from:
  `${GABINETE_ACERVO}\NUCLEO 4.0\02 - MODELO - DESPACHO - INICIAL E REDISTRIBUICAO.docx`
- If the origin court already ruled on gratuity, tutela, citation, or remessa, the new act from the Nucleo must expressly acknowledge that prior ruling instead of behaving like a fresh untouched filing.
- Preserve, when supported by the dossier, the standard gabinete opening sequence for this subtype:
  1. science of the redistribution and of the relevant ruling from the origin court;
  2. science of pending habilitation and patron-annotation requests;
  3. the standard `+Acordo` paragraph.
- In the `+Acordo` paragraph, do not add that the author manifested express disfavor toward conciliation unless that exact manifestation appears in the dossier. If the file is silent, keep the non-design rationale institutional and do not impute a procedural statement to the party.
- More broadly, in the initial-order workflow, do not attribute to any party a request, statement, factual assertion, agreement, disagreement, admission, waiver, or preference unless that content is expressly traceable in the dossier.
- In passive litisconsortium, separate each defendant's status before drafting. If only one defendant appeared or contested, regularize only that defendant and address the other defendant's pending citation, deadline, impediment, or defense separately.
- If there is a pending petition from one defendant asking for devolucao de prazo, justo impedimento, or technical access regularization, that petition must be expressly decided before a generic replica/proof-order is issued.
- If tutela is still pending, pair with `$tutela-urgencia-tjrj`.

## Justice gratuity
- If the dossier shows sufficient support and no relevant contradiction, defer.
- If there is doubt or missing financial support, apply art. 99, paragrafo 2o, of the CPC and Sumula 39 of the TJRJ, demanding concrete documents.
- If the financial incompatibility is evident, deny the benefit in a reasoned way and determine cost payment.
