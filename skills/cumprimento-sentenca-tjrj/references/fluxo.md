# Fluxo

## Required checks
1. Identify the title being enforced and the exact current stage.
2. Confirm whether the debtor was already intimated to pay or comply.
3. Check whether there is impugnacao, objection, payment proof, partial satisfaction, or a pending coercive request.
4. Choose the exact act needed now:
   - initial compliance order
   - ruling on impugnacao
   - obligation-to-do enforcement
   - coercive step
   - satisfaction recognition
   - extinction

## Drafting cautions
- Do not reuse a generic cumprimento order when the record is already in the impugnacao phase.
- Mention the exact prior command that is being enforced or challenged.
