# Impugnacao And Astreintes

## Impugnacao
- Check timeliness, the exact grounds raised, and whether the issue is excess, inexigibility, wrong calculation, or another post-judgment defense.
- Face the debtor's arguments directly instead of answering with a generic continuation order.

## Obligation to do and astreintes
- Check the prior order, proof of intimation, actual compliance evidence, and whether the right response is maintenance, increase, reduction, substitution, or removal of the fine.
- If the debtor partially complied, tailor the next command to the missing part only.

## Fazenda Publica note
- If the cumprimento is against Fazenda Publica, verify whether the proper path is RPV, precatorio, or the specific public-law enforcement regime before drafting.
