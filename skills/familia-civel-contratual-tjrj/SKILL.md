# Familia Civel Contratual TJRJ

## When to use
- Use when the material core is a private-law contractual or obligational dispute that should not be treated primarily under the CDC.
- Use for cobranca, inadimplemento contratual, rescisao, alugueres, ajuste empresarial, and similar ordinary civil matters.

## Workflow
1. Read [guia.md](references/guia.md).
2. Confirm that the case should be framed primarily under Civil Code and CPC logic, not under a consumer-law matrix.
3. Preserve the contractual model architecture and keep the reasoning tied to the decisive documentary anchors.
4. Avoid importing CDC language by inertia when the dossier does not justify it.

## Output expectation
- The draft must read as an ordinary civil-contract ruling with clean contractual framing.
- Keep contractual breach, performance, default, and evidentiary sequence explicit in the reasoning.
