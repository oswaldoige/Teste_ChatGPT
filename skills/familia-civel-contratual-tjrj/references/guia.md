# Guia da Familia Civel Contratual

## Trigger checklist
- The dispute revolves around contract formation, performance, breach, rescission, payment, delivery, accounting, or ordinary obligational consequences.
- The dossier does not justify a predominantly consumer-law or public-law matrix.

## Typical consequences
- Emphasize contract, title, notice, payment, performance, breach, and damages proof.
- Preserve the order of factual demonstration from the canonical model.
- Keep legal framing primarily in the Civil Code and CPC, with sectoral statutes only when the dossier requires them.
- When a stable gabinete block exists in `90_BLOCOS_REUTILIZAVEIS`, preserve that opening or burden-of-proof bridge instead of rewriting it from scratch.

## Frequent subfamilies
- Cobranca contratual
- Rescisao e perdas e danos
- Locacao e encargos
- Prestacao de servicos entre particulares sem matriz consumerista dominante
- Ajustes empresariais e obrigacionais comuns
