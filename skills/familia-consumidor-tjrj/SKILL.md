# Familia Consumidor TJRJ

## When to use
- Use when the material core of the dispute is a consumer relationship.
- Use for bank, health, private education, service-failure, product-defect, billing, and similar CDC-centered disputes.

## Workflow
1. Read [guia.md](references/guia.md).
2. Confirm whether the dossier, the prior rulings, or the adherent model support CDC treatment.
3. Preserve the canonical consumer architecture of the chosen model instead of rewriting the reasoning from scratch.
4. Make explicit, when pertinent, the consumer/supplier positions and the immediate consequences of that classification.

## Output expectation
- The draft must read as a consumer-law act, not merely as a generic civil ruling with occasional CDC citations.
- When the model and the dossier support it, the reasoning should expressly mention that the legal relationship is one of consumption.
