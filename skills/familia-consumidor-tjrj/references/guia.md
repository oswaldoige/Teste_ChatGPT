# Guia da Familia Consumidor

## Trigger checklist
- The controversy arises from a product or service supplied in the market.
- One party occupies the position of consumer and the other that of supplier.
- The dossier already invokes CDC, a prior ruling recognized CDC, or the best aderent model is consumer-based.

## Typical consequences
- Express identification of consumer and supplier positions.
- Duty of information and good-faith analysis when the factual matrix depends on communication failure, opacity, or operational omission.
- Objective liability or burden-shifting where the dossier and the phase permit it.
- Preservation of the consumer paragraph from the base model whenever it exists and remains pertinent.
- When a stable gabinete opening exists in `90_BLOCOS_REUTILIZAVEIS`, preserve that wording with only the factual adaptations required by the concrete case.
- In sentences, treat the burden-of-proof topic with procedural precision: do not newly defer inversion of the burden of proof in the judgment. Either mention a prior interlocutory ruling that already addressed the issue or state that a fresh ruling on the request is unnecessary or prejudiced at the sentencing stage.

## Frequent subfamilies
- Banking and Pix disputes
- Health plan and treatment disputes
- Private education and university operational disputes
- Service-failure and billing disputes
- Product-defect and delivery-performance disputes
