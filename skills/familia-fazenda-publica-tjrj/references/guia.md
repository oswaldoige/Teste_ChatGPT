# Guia da Familia Fazenda Publica

## Trigger checklist
- The passive or active side includes Fazenda Publica or the legal regime is materially administrative or statutory.
- The controversy depends on public-law limits for tutela, proof, payment, implementation, or consectarios.

## Typical consequences
- Check legal regime, reversibility, separation of powers concerns, and administrative feasibility.
- Keep statutory hooks visible in the reasoning.
- Adjust dispositive consequences, implementation cadence, and sucumbencia to the applicable public-law framework.
- When a stable gabinete block exists in `90_BLOCOS_REUTILIZAVEIS`, preserve that structure instead of flattening the reasoning into generic prose.

## Frequent subfamilies
- Servidor publico e remuneracao
- Obrigacao de fazer contra ente publico
- Demandas de saude com ente publico
- Cobranca ou diferencas estatutarias
- Litigios administrativos em geral
