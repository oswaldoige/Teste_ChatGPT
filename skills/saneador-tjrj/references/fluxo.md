# Fluxo

## Required checks
1. Confirm all relevant contestations and replies were reviewed.
2. Resolve or at least frame the pending preliminary issues.
3. Decide whether expert evidence is needed.
4. Delimit the controverted points and the evidentiary burden.
5. Define the next procedural step with precision.

## Drafting rule
- A saneadora is not a placeholder. It must reflect the actual evidentiary needs of the case and the true post-contraditorio state of the record.
