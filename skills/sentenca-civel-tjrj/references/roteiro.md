# Roteiro

## Maturity check before drafting
- Confirm there is no unresolved embargos de declaracao, pending contradiction issue, or missing citation problem.
- Confirm the last relevant decision and all later petitions were reviewed.
- Confirm the case is truly ready for judgment and not for a replica, saneador, or evidentiary measure.
- If the instrutory phase was already closed by audiencia, saneadora, or express order that the autos should return `conclusos para sentença`, do not draft a mere `despacho de retorno conclusos` after the transit or communication of an incidental appeal. In that scenario, the next act is the sentence unless a new unresolved incident truly arose afterward.
- When oral proof was deferred to the plaintiff and the plaintiff later fails to appear at the AIJ, treat the oral proof as not produced and decide whether the documentary record is already sufficient for judgment, instead of automatically reopening the phase.

## Model and consequence search
- Start with:
  `modelos`
- After selecting the best canonical model, open the matching folder in:
  `blocos`
- Then move to `00_PRIORITARIOS_AUTOMACAO` and the best recurring-family models only if the local nucleus is insufficient.
- If there is a highly aderent family model, draft from that model's skeleton instead of creating a fresh sentence architecture. This includes the same kind of opening (`Trata-se...` when present), procedural-report sequencing, transition formulas (`É o relatório. Decido.`), order of legal discussion, dispositive cadence, and close.
- In recurring public-law salary families such as piso nacional do magistério, treat the best family model as the default matriz. The sentence should look like a concretized version of that model, not a stylistically independent rewrite.
- In recurring consumer-service families, if the selected model opens the merits with an express statement that the relationship is one of consumption, preserve that bridge in the final reasoning. Do not replace it with only indirect references to good faith, information duties, or later mentions of the CDC in the dispositive.
- When the local reusable blocks contain a standard gabinete opening for the family, import that opening with minimal factual adaptation instead of paraphrasing it into generic prose.
- When a saneadora or prior decision already recognized the CDC or inverted the burden of proof, and the sentence family model usually reincorporates that premise in the reasoning, restate it expressly in the merits section with the concrete `ID` reference when relevant.
- In consumer sentences, do not newly grant inversion of the burden of proof in the judgment itself. If the dossier shows a prior interlocutory ruling on the point, report that ruling with the proper `ID` or `Evento + sigla`. If there was no prior ruling and the case is already mature for judgment, treat the specific request as unnecessary or prejudiced at this stage and decide the merits from the concrete evidentiary distribution instead of writing `defiro a inversao do onus da prova`.

## Output rule
- Always deliver complete and concise `.docx` versions for sentences.
- The concise version may compress supporting discussion, but it must still preserve the same recognizable family structure and gabinete phrasing of the selected base model.
- Before delivery, reopen each generated `.docx` and confirm there is no mojibake or in-word question mark replacing PT-BR characters.
