# Subject Families

This file is a second-layer refiner. First classify the case into the macrofamilia `consumidor`, `civel contratual`, or `Fazenda Publica`, and only then use the subject cues below.

## Bank or Pix fraud
- Check whether the requested measure is preservation of data, MED-related action, suspension of charges, or account blocking.
- Avoid attributing immediate liability to all defendants without a factual link.

## Health
- Check prescription, urgency, continuity of treatment, and whether the requested relief is reversible enough for this stage.

## Vehicle and DETRAN disputes
- A transfer restriction is often safer than a circulation restriction at the first moment.
- Consider DETRAN information requests and chain-of-title reconstruction before broader asset restraints.

## Water and sewer / 3o Nucleo 4.0
- Use the specialized models and jurisprudence pack from the Nucleo 4.0 folder.
- Separate billing suspension, refaturamento, inspection, and supply-restoration issues.

## Fazenda Publica
- Check the public-law regime, reversibility, and whether the measure is compatible with the procedural posture.

## Descumprimento of prior relief
- Identify the exact prior order, the proof of intimation, the compliance evidence, and whether the correct response is enforcement, clarification, or astreinte adjustment.
