# Fluxo

## Required sequence
1. Identify the exact urgent measures requested.
2. Check whether all requests share the same legal basis or should be split.
3. Apply art. 300 of the CPC with specific analysis of probability, danger, and reversibility.
4. Prefer partial relief when only part of the request meets the standard.
5. Calibrate astreintes, deadlines, and scope of compliance.
6. Individualize commands by defendant when there is litisconsorcio.

## Drafting cautions
- Use `Evento + sigla` or `ID` when the factual anchor matters.
- Do not convert a limited protective measure into a broad anticipatory judgment.
- In descumprimento or complement requests based on faturas, contas, or telas de cobrança, verify the underlying document before stating the exact name of a rubric or line item. If the wording is not clearly visible in the document, draft the order with neutral language such as `cobranca equivalente vinculada ao debito controvertido`, and attribute any specific label to the party or to the prior ruling that used it.
- If the order is complex, create complete and concise `.docx` versions.
